Recruitment completed

“sufficient capacity available of certified forensic staff for handling major incidents (e.g., internal staff or contracting an external forensic team)”

9 Risks, Priorities & Decisions Required Source · Executive decisions

Achievements, Risks & Next Steps

The funding decision is made. From here the constraint is procurement speed and internal capacity — and that is where support from the Executive Committee is needed.

Achievements

  • Documentation grown from 13 to 57 cybersecurity documents — 44 new, approximately 338% growth.
  • DFIR / SOC L2 Specialist recruitment completed; joining 1 September 2026.
  • Six of twelve initiatives moved into delivery.
  • FY2026 cybersecurity budget approved · SAR 665,000.
  • 57 cybersecurity documents developed and maintained; 26 formally approved.
  • Secure disposal genuinely covered — BitRaser hardware plus DiPu destruction service.

Risks

  • 31 of 57 cybersecurity documents are not yet formally approved.
  • Testing and assurance pricing came in higher than expected.
  • Without certified forensic capacity, a major incident cannot be properly investigated or evidenced to the regulator.
  • Security architecture may be corrected after the fact rather than designed up front while the architect role is unfilled.
  • Strong architect candidates may be lost without prompt headcount and offer approval.
  • Delivery is constrained by contracting speed and internal capacity.

Dependencies

  • Network Detection and Response cannot come before network segmentation.
  • Red teaming follows the first assessment round.
  • Stage 3 items follow the dependencies above rather than assigned dates.
  • Vendor selection and contracting gate all Stage 2 items.
  • Forensic capability depends on the specialist joining on 1 September 2026.
  • Architect recruitment depends on headcount confirmation and offer approval.

Next steps

  • Convert the approved budget into signed contracts and working capability.
  • Complete vendor selection and contracting for Stage 2 items.
  • Progress document approval and publication.
  • Onboard the DFIR / SOC L2 Specialist from 1 September 2026.
  • Complete Cybersecurity Architect recruitment through to offer.
  • Report spend against the approved envelope quarterly, by category.

Decisions required from the Executive Committee

1

Confirm headcount for the Cybersecurity Architect

Headcount confirmation and support at offer stage are required so that strong candidates are not lost.

2

Support procurement and contracting speed

With budget approved, delivery depends on vendor selection, contracting speed and internal capacity — not on further financial decisions.

3

Endorse approval of the governance documentation

31 of the 57 developed documents are not yet formally approved; approval and publication is an active Stage 1 workstream.

The funding decision is made. Spend will be reported against the approved envelope quarterly so the Committee can see money turning into controls.
