09 · Resourcing

Two Roles Required by the SAMA Framework

Digital Forensics & Incident Response Specialist
Completed
SAMA CSF 3.3.15-4.c · Cyber Security Incident Management
“sufficient capacity available of certified forensic staff for handling major incidents (e.g., internal staff or contracting an external forensic team)”

WHY IT MATTERSWithout certified forensic capacity, Muhlah cannot properly investigate a major incident, preserve evidence, or demonstrate to the regulator what happened.

DFIR / SOC L2 Specialist recruitment has been completed.
The specialist will join Muhlah on 1 September 2026.
Cybersecurity Architect
In recruitment
SAMA CSF 3.3.4-3.c · Cyber Security Architecture
“the requirement of having qualified cyber security architects”

WHY IT MATTERSSecurity architecture must be designed and documented before major projects — segmentation, NDR and cloud changes — rather than corrected afterwards.

Candidates are being interviewed.
Headcount and offer approval are required to complete this hire.

Both roles address named requirements in the SAMA Cybersecurity Framework. DFIR / SOC L2 recruitment is complete with a confirmed start date of 1 September 2026; architect recruitment is in progress, and headcount and offer approval are required to complete it.
